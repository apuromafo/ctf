# Lab 09: XEN

## 🎯 Mission / Misión
Humongous Retail operates a nationwide chain of stores. The company has reacted to several recent skimming incidents by investing heavily in their POS systems. Keen to avoid any further negative publicity, they have engaged the services of a penetration testing company to assess the security of their perimeter and internal infrastructure.

## 🔍 Description / Descripción
Xen is designed to put your skills in enumeration, breakout, lateral movement, and privilege escalation to the test within a small Active Directory environment. The goal is to gain a foothold on the internal network, escalate privileges and ultimately compromise the domain while collecting several flags along the way.

## 👤 Target / Objetivo
XEN is designed for junior penetration testers who are looking to start practising their skills and senior penetration testers in search of a lab with legacy systems that showcases a scenario still relevant to the real world.

## 🛠️ Skills Required / Habilidades
- [ ] Basic knowledge of penetration testing tools
- [ ] Basic understanding of Windows operating systems
- [ ] Basic understanding of networking
- [ ] Basic knowledge of Active Directory

## 🏆 Learning Outcomes / Resultados
- [ ] Enumeration
- [ ] Lateral Movement
- [ ] Privilege Escalation
- [ ] Pivoting
- [ ] Phishing techniques
- [ ] Situational awareness
- [ ] Active Directory enumeration and exploitation
