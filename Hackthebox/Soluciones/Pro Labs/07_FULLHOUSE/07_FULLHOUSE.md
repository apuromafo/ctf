# Lab 07: FULLHOUSE

## 🎯 Mission / Misión
FullHouse manages the HTBCasino and is strongly committed to ensuring the privacy and security of its players. Therefore, the casino has enlisted your expertise to identify and report potential vulnerabilities in both new and legacy components. The objective is to establish a foothold on the internal network, escalate privileges, and ultimately enhance the overall infrastructure security, all while uncovering various flags. This lab is designed to test your skills in Enumeration, Code Review, Pivoting, Web Exploitation, and other attacking techniques.

## 🔍 Description / Descripción
FullHouse is an intermediate-level real-world simulation lab that introduces participants to blockchain, artificial intelligence, and machine learning attacks. However, it is not limited to common network penetration testing and active directory misconfigurations.

## 👤 Target / Objetivo
FullHouse is designed for junior penetration testers who are looking to enhance their skill set, and senior penetration testers in search of a challenging lab in order to hone their engagement skills.
