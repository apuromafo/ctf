# Lab 08: ORION

## 🎯 Mission / Misión
Orion Ltd is a small enterprise looking to strengthen its security posture, and has tasked you with assessing their network perimeter and internal infrastructure by running a full penetration test. The goal of this challenging lab is to gain a foothold, elevate privileges and move laterally, in order to reach the goal of domain admin.

## 🔍 Description / Descripción
Orion is a small lab meant as a stepping stone towards beginner-level Pro Labs such as Genesis and Dante, to which it serves as a nice introduction. Most (but not all) of the attack vectors are similar in both scope and complexity to the ones found in Genesis, but on a smaller scale; pivoting, which is found in Dante, is also introduced.

## 👤 Target / Objetivo
Orion is designed for people who are getting started in the field of Cyber Security, and for people with some previous Penetration Testing experience that want to test their skills on a small-sized network before tackling the bigger Pro Labs.

## 🛠️ Skills Required / Habilidades
- [ ] Basic knowledge of penetration testing tools
- [ ] Basic understanding of Windows and Linux operating systems
- [ ] Basic understanding of networking

## 🏆 Learning Outcomes / Resultados
- [ ] Enumeration
- [ ] Public Exploit Research
- [ ] Lateral Movement
- [ ] Privilege Escalation
- [ ] Pivoting
- [ ] Active Directory
