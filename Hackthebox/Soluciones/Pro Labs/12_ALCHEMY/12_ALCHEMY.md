# Lab 12: ALCHEMY

## 🎯 Mission / Misión
Alchemy LLC has been contracted by Sogard Brewing Co. to evaluate the security of their recently established brewery factory. The primary aim of this collaboration is to fortify the factory against potential cyber threats, ensuring the safety, security, and reliability of its operations. A crucial aspect of this initiative is the integration of Information Technology (IT) networks with Operational Technology (OT) infrastructure. a move designed to enhance the oversight, control, and efficiency of the brewery
