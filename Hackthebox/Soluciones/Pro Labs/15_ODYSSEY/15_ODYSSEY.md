# Lab 15: ODYSSEY

## 🎯 Mission / Misión
Odyssey gaming is a rapidly growing startup. Due to high demand and value in their gaming services, they have decided to hire you to perform penetration testing on their Infrastructure.

## 🔍 Description / Descripción
Odyssey is designed to put your skills in enumeration, reversing, VoIP exploitation and much more. The goal is to gain a foothold on the internal network, escalate privileges and ultimately compromise the entire gaming Infrastructure while collecting several flags along the way.

## 👤 Target / Objetivo
Odyssey is designed to test your skills on multiple domains like VoIP, Oxide, NTLM stealing, PDF malware and much more, its for people who enjoy complex interaction between technologies to find loopholes along side with Kubernetes cluster exploitation and solaris Operating System misconfigurations; this lab also caters to those looking to push the boundaries of their knowledge and experience in multiple domains and sophisticated security scenarios.

## 🛠️ Skills Required / Habilidades
- [ ] A grasp of penetration testing methodologies
- [ ] An understanding of the Linux, Solaris and Windows operating systems
- [ ] Knowledge in multiple domains
- [ ] Basic programming knowledge
- [ ] Basic understand of VoIP and other custom technologies
