# Lab 16: ASCENSION

## 🎯 Mission / Misión
Daedalus Airlines is quickly becoming a major player in global aviation. However, the pace of growth has meant that the company has accumulated a lot of technical debt. In order to avoid a data breach and potentially put their supply chain at risk, Daedalus have hired your Cyber Security firm to test their systems.

## 🔍 Description / Descripción
Ascension is designed to test your skills in Enumeration, Exploitation, Pivoting, Forest Traversal and Privilege Escalation inside two small Active Directory networks. The goal is to gain access to the trusted partner, pivot through the network and compromise two Active Directory forests while collecting several flags along the way. Can you Ascend?

## 👤 Target / Objetivo
Ascension is designed for advanced penetration testers looking to hone their skills in complex environments involving Active Directory enumeration, domain pivoting, and privilege escalation. The lab offers a variety of challenges that test your ability to exploit vulnerabilities across multiple systems. With a focus on lateral movement, credential harvesting, and advanced AD exploitation techniques, Ascension provides a realistic experience for those aiming to push their expertise in multi-domain scenarios and sophisticated attack vectors.

## 🛠️ Skills Required / Habilidades
- [ ] Basic knowledge of penetration testing tools
- [ ] Basic understanding of Windows operating systems
- [ ] Basic understanding of networking
- [ ] Basic knowledge of Active Directory

## 🏆 Learning Outcomes / Resultados

- [ ] Web Application attacks
- [ ] Active Directory Exploitation
- [ ] Credential Harvesting and Abuse
- [ ] Network Pivoting and Lateral Movement
- [ ] Privilege Escalation Strategies
- [ ] Persistence and Post-Exploitation Tactics
