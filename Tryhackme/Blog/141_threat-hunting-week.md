## What is threat hunting?
Threat hunting is the proactive process of searching for signs of malicious activities or potential security threats within an organisation's network or systems. Unlike traditional cybersecurity measures that rely on predefined rules and signatures, threat hunting is more akin to a cyber investigation.
## Why is threat hunting important?
In today's digital age, cyber threats are evolving at an unprecedented rate. Threat actors are becoming increasingly sophisticated, making it crucial for organisations to take a proactive approach to cyber security. Threat hunting plays a pivotal role in this regard, helping to identify and neutralise threats before they cause significant damage. It's like being a digital detective, always one step ahead of the cyber criminals 🕵️
