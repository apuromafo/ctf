## Advent of Cyber ‘Side Quest’ Winners
As part of this year's Advent of Cyber, we created a side quest for our most advanced users. The side quest challenges ranged from hard to insane difficulties and were designed for those of you who wanted something more complex than the main AoC track. While we expected some users to participate, the reception we received was overwhelmingly positive and greatly exceeded our expectations. A huge thank you to all of you for participating! Here's a quick recap of the event:
* A total of 3694 users joined the side quest to help the Yeti
* 1208 completed at least one of the SQ challenges
* 21 users got the coveted SNOW/OFF badge for completing ALL challenges
* The phrase "**no hints**" was used more than 1000 times in our Discord channel for the event
* More than a dozen memes were created in the process
* At least six different bypasses to the intended solutions were found, two of which were 0-day vulnerabilities!Congratulations to the side quest winner, **Aquinas**, for being the first to complete all challenges. The competition was tough, having **moo** in second place and sending their last flag just 5 minutes later. The third place goes to **jaxafed**, who followed them closely.We hope you enjoyed the challenges as much as we enjoyed creating them. It was great to see you all participating, assembling teams and putting so much effort into helping the Yeti succeed in his plan. Your feedback is also important to keep improving the event, so remember to fill in the feedback survey if you participated.Please note that the Side Quest was a separate event from Advent of Cyber, meaning that completing tasks from the Side Quest will not increase your chances of winning from the main Advent of Cyber $50,000 prize draw. Completing the Side Quest was entirely optional and just for fun!
