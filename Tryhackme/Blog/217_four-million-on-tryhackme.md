# Four Million Users on TryHackMe!

We’re celebrating a monumental milestone: **TryHackMe has officially reached 4 million users! **This achievement reflects not just the growth of our platform, but also the increasing global recognition of the importance of cyber security education. Let’s take a look at how TryHackMe has grown over the years, the incredible journey we’ve embarked on together, and everything we have in store for the coming months!
## Our journey to four million
Founded in 2018, TryHackMe was built with a clear goal: to make cyber security education accessible to everyone.From day one, our mission has been to break down the barriers to cyber security knowledge. We strive to provide affordable, high-quality training that empowers individuals to start or advance their careers in cyber security. Our users come from all walks of life, proving that anyone, regardless of background, can learn and excel in this field! And this is evident in our [user success stories](https://tryhackme.com/r/resources/success-story?ref=blog.tryhackme.com).This year alone, we celebrated hitting 3 million users — and now, just months later, we’ve reached 4 million!
## Thank you!
As we reflect on this incredible milestone, we want to express our deepest gratitude to every user who has been part of this journey. Whether you’ve been with us from the beginning or just joined, you are a vital part of the TryHackMe story.The TryHackMe community is at the heart of this achievement. Every user who logs into the platform, completes a room, participates in a CTF, or shares their learning journey on social media contributes to our collective success. Your passion and dedication is what continues to drive us!Here’s to empowering millions more learners, breaking barriers, and making the cyber security world a more skilled and inclusive place. Together, we’re not just learning; we’re shaping the future of cyber security.**To celebrate this milestone, we’re excited to have just officially released ‘dark mode’ across the TryHackMe platform!**
## What’s next for TryHackMe?
Hitting 4 million users is only just the beginning. We’re committed to continuing our mission of making cyber security education accessible and engaging, and becoming the best cyber security training platform out there. In the next couple of months, you can expect new paths around incident response and advanced red teaming, major upgrades to existing paths, and access to our (premium &amp; B2B only) SOC Simulator product. Meanwhile, we have some very exciting updates coming to the platform in early 2025, helping you learn faster and better. And of course, much more 😉Once again, thank you for being part of the TryHackMe community!
