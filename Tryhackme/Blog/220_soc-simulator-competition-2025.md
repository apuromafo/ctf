## How to Get Started
Registration opens on January 21, 2025, and you’ll have until 11:59 PM (UTC) on January 31, 2025, to complete the two scenarios. Only team members who achieve a 100% completion rate in both scenarios will qualify for the prizes.Here’s a quick checklist to get started:
* Assemble your team with a minimum of five members from your company.
* Register your team or join an existing team dashboard starting January 21.
* Complete the 'Phishing Unfolding' and 'Initial Drift' scenarios and climb the leaderboard! You can see where your team stacks up on the competition tracker.
## **Team Dashboard FAQ**

## **Prerequisites**

* **Email Requirement**: You must use a work email to join or create a team dashboard. Public email domains like Gmail or Hotmail are not supported.
* **Email Verification**: Ensure your email is verified on TryHackMe.
## **Creating a Team Dashboard**

* **Access Team Dashboard Page**: Navigate to the team dashboard [page](https://tryhackme.com/r/workspace?ref=blog.tryhackme.com) on TryHackMe.
* **Enter Team Dashboard Name**: If your organisation's team dashboard hasn't been created, you'll be prompted to enter its name. Note that team dashboard names are not unique.
* **Domain-Based**: Team dashboards are organised by email domain. For example, emails with @tryhackme.com will be grouped together.
## **Inviting Members**

* **Automatic Grouping**: Members are automatically grouped into team dashboards based on their email domain. There is no manual invitation process.
## **Joining an Existing Team Dashboard**

* **Verify Email**: Ensure your email is verified on TryHackMe.
* **Automatic Assignment**: Upon verification, you'll be automatically assigned to the team dashboard corresponding to your email domain.
## **Important Notes**

* **Non-Transferable**: Once joined, you cannot leave or switch team dashboards.
* **Email Changes**: Changing your email keeps you in the same team dashboard but may restrict access to the page.
## **Features Enhancing Collaboration and Competition**

* **Leaderboard**: Tracks top scorers and resets monthly.
* **Challenges**: Participate in team challenges to foster collaboration.
* **Activity Tracking**: View recent activities and achievements within the team.
