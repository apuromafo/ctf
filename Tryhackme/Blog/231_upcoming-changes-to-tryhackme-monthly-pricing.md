At TryHackMe, we are passionate about making cybersecurity education accessible, hands-on, and constantly evolving to meet the needs of our learners. Over the past year, we have been blown away by the enthusiasm and dedication of our community. Your commitment inspires us to keep building, improving, and delivering the best possible learning experience.Over the past year, we’ve invested heavily in expanding and improving TryHackMe. We’ve added over 343 rooms—Over 30% increase in content! This means even more opportunities to learn the latest cybersecurity skills, practice real-world scenarios, and prepare for industry certifications.We want to be transparent with you about some upcoming changes to our pricing and explain why these changes are necessary to ensure TryHackMe remains a leading platform for cyber security training.
## **What’s Changing?**
Starting **June 16th, 2025**, the price of our **monthly subscription will increase** for both existing and new subscribers. The **annual subscription price will remain unchanged, **we believe this plan continues to offer the best value for our committed learners.
## Why Is the Annual Plan Staying the Same?
We believe our annual subscription offers the best value for our users. By keeping the annual price unchanged, we’re rewarding our long-term learners and making it easier for you to invest in your cyber security journey for the year ahead. If you’re currently on a monthly plan, upgrading to an annual subscription before June 16th will lock in your savings and shield you from the upcoming price increase.
## How to Lock In the Best Value
If you’re an existing monthly subscriber, you can **switch to the annual plan before June 16th** to secure the current lower rate for the next year. Not only will you avoid the price increase, but you’ll also **benefit from the equivalent of two and half months free** compared to paying monthly.
## Our Commitment to You
We’re committed to delivering the most comprehensive, hands-on cybersecurity training available. Your subscription directly supports the creation of new content, platform improvements, and a vibrant community that helps learners at every stage.We’re grateful for your continued support and feedback. If you have any questions about your subscription, please reach out to [support@tryhackme.com](mailto:support@tryhackme.com) we’re here to help.Thank you for being part of the TryHackMe community and for trusting us with your learning journey.
