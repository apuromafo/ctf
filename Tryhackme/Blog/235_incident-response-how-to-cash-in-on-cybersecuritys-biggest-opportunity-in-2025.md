**Daily Practice (1-2 hours):**

* Study network traffic patterns from malware communications**Key Milestone:** Recognise brute force attacks, malware infections, and data exfiltration patterns without guidance.**Tools to Master:** Splunk fundamentals, Wireshark basics, Windows Event Viewer
## **Week 5-8: Investigation Methodology**
**Daily Practice (2-3 hours):**
* Complete TryHackMe incident response rooms with time constraints

## **Week 9-12: Communication and Coordination**
