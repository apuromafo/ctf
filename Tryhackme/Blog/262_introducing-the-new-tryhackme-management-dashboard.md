# Introducing the New TryHackMe Management Dashboard

## Smarter visibility. Stronger insights. Streamlined control.
We’re excited to introduce the **revamped TryHackMe Management Dashboard**, built to give security leaders and managers a **faster, clearer view** of team performance and engagement across training initiatives.This update focuses on delivering **data-driven insights**, **simplified workflows**, and **enhanced usability** for administrators managing cybersecurity skill development at scale.
## **A Clearer Overview at a Glance**
The redesigned dashboard presents the key information you need—right where you expect it.
* **Active Users:** Track license usage and pending invites in real time.
* **Assignment Completion Rates:** Instantly view progress across teams.
* **Questions Answered:** Monitor hands-on learning engagement.
## **Visualise Skills Growth and Progress**
Gain deeper insight into your team’s evolving skills profile.The new **Skills Progression Graph** allows you to track growth over time by role, skill category, or specific time frame—helping you identify strengths and areas that need attention.
## **Track Assignments with Precision**
Keep learning initiatives on track with a dedicated assignment section that highlights what’s complete, pending, or overdue.This gives managers an instant view into **who’s learning, what’s lagging, and where intervention is needed**.
## **Encourage Engagement with Leaderboards**
Drive motivation and celebrate achievement.New **team leaderboards** spotlight top performers, creating healthy competition and engagement across your cybersecurity teams.
## **Smarter User Management and Onboarding**
We’ve completely redesigned how administrators can add and manage users.The new flow lets you onboard large cohorts and assign training instantly—helping teams start faster.
## Key improvements:

* **Bulk CSV Uploads** to import multiple users at once
* **Immediate Group Placement** for new learners
* **Auto-Assignment of Training** based on team or skill focus
## **Smarter Assignments from Day One**
Admins can now:
* Place new users directly into existing groups to inherit active assignments.
* Auto-suggest learning paths based on team skill focus.
* Create new groups and assign modules in a single step.
## Flexible User Onboarding
Admins can still add users one by one, but now with more control:
* Select roles when adding users
* Tag skills to guide training recommendations
* Place users directly into existing groups or create new groups directly for these new added users**Why These Updates Matter**The enhanced Management Dashboard is designed for **time efficiency, clarity, and scalability.**Managers can now:
* Measure training performance with real-time analytics
* Streamline onboarding and assignment setup
* Track measurable progress aligned with security readiness goalsThis release reflects our commitment to **empowering security leaders** to make informed, data-backed decisions in workforce development.These updates are available now for all TryHackMe Business accounts.
