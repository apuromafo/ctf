Since first launching, we’ve reached almost two million users on TryHackMe, and have seen tremendous growth through our pathway launches, room releases, and hacker events. To keep bringing you epic content, we continue to expand our internal team and, as such, have some exciting [career opportunities](https://tryhackme.com/careers?ref=blog.tryhackme.com) to join us on our journey!Discover what it’s like to work at TryHackMe, and hear from some of our experts working behind the scenes.
## Growth &amp; Innovation
We’ve had an incredible journey of growth since launching, and it’s all down to our team and community. To accompany this growth and continue making a world-class interactive platform, we’ve doubled our employee headcount in the last year and will continue to expand our team through some exciting opportunities.We attribute our growth to our legendary team at TryHackMe who help solidify TryHackMe as the most innovative cyber training platform, and the incredible tribe of learners who are loyal to our training!
## Career Progression
With a growing need to upskill in all areas of cyber security, career progression and upskilling are at the core of everything we do.That’s why, at TryHackMe, every employee is allocated a minimum annual training budget of £2,500, to use towards online courses, acquiring certifications, strengthening your skills, or even branching out to achieve new goals! Alongside personal development, devote 10% of your working time to any project that will benefit you in your role.
## Team Events
In a remote working world, our international team can be found in all corners of the globe. We’re always connected through team events, with fortnightly team lunches paid for by us, team-building activities, frequent socials, departmental meet-ups to collaborate, and a regular TryHackathon.As a way to reward all staff, our annual retreat unites our global team, face-to-face, with an opportunity to collaborate and have fun. Our last company retreat saw us visit the vibrant city of Lisbon, for tuk-tuk rides, segway tours, paintballing, treasure hunts, sunset dinners on the beach, finished off by our company awards night, plus much much more!
