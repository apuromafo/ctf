What a year it’s been! As we bid farewell to another remarkable year at TryHackMe, it’s the perfect moment to reflect on the milestones, achievements, and the incredible journey that has defined our 2023.We’ve welcomed over a million new users in the last 12 months alone, and earlier this year, even reached a whopping two million users. With more users joining us on this journey daily, this number continues to grow, bringing the total TryHackMe user count to just over 2.6 million! Let’s have a look back at some of our highlights from 2023…
