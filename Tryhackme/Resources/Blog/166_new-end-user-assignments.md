TryHackMe is excited to announce the launch of our **new** Assignments area for users in Business and Education seats. As the first user-centric feature we have released, this is a game-changing update for all users in a B2B dashboard and the first of many exciting new updates to come.
## What is the End User Assignments page?
As a new area for Business and Education users to view their Assignments, **the new page shows all of your existing Assignments, including links to the rooms, deadline dates and status tracking**.Previously, when users were given Assignments by their Managers and Tutors there was nowhere to view or keep track of what Assignments you had in progress. Instead, users were required to keep track of the email notification sent to them with each Assignment. This led to poor Assignment completion rates amongst users and frustration when trying to organise their training.With the **new** product area launching exclusively for Business and Education users today, they will no longer have to go searching their mailboxes for Assignment links and to check deadline dates. Now, users can simply navigate to their Assignments page (underneath the ‘Learn’ tab) to view all of their existing and past Assignments in one place. Within the page, users will be able to track:
* Each Assignment given to them, with a direct link to the room/content assigned
* The deadline date for each assignment
* Their progress on the Assignment
* The current status of each Assignment from ‘Not Started’, ‘In Progress’, ‘Due Soon’, ‘Overdue’ &amp; ‘Complete’.Users can filter their Assignments based on status for effortless organisation, helping them to keep on top of their workloads.“Business and Education users will be thrilled with this update,” says Chris Hartwell, Customer Success Manager at TryHackMe. “Students and Professionals alike now have a really convenient place to manage all of their Assignments, whilst Managers and Tutors will additionally benefit from better Assignment completion rates and less administration, directing their team and students to the right place.”Not already a Business or Education user?
