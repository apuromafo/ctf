## **The Problem: High Costs in Cybersecurity Training**
Many learners believe the only way to get into the field is to enrol in an expensive bootcamp or degree program. And while those can provide structure, they’re often out of reach for:
* **Career changers** who can’t quit their job to study full-time
* **Students** balancing debt with their desire to up-skill
* **Global learners** in regions where salaries don’t align with $10,000 training programs👉 Reports show cybersecurity bootcamps often cost **$3,000–$13,000+** ([Cybersecurity Guide](https://cybersecurityguide.org/bootcamps/?ref=blog.tryhackme.com)). Compare that to the cost of a monthly training subscription and the difference is striking.
## **What “Depth” Really Means in Cybersecurity**
It’s not enough to just read about vulnerabilities or memorise definitions. Employers want to see that you can:
* Investigate real incidents
* Use industry tools like SIEMs and packet analysers
* Understand attacker techniques and defensive strategies
* Write reports and communicate findings clearly**Depth = hands-on experience.** Without practice, theory doesn’t translate into employable skills.
