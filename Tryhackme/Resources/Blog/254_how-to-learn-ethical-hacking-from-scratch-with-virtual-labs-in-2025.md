## **What Is Ethical Hacking?**
At its core, ethical hacking means using the same techniques as malicious hackers—**but with permission**. Ethical hackers test systems, applications, and networks to uncover vulnerabilities before criminals exploit them.Common roles that use ethical hacking skills include:
* **Penetration testers** – simulate real attacks to uncover weaknesses
* **Red teamers** – emulate advanced adversaries across entire environments
* **Bug bounty hunters** – legally test applications for rewards💡 The key difference from criminal hacking: **consent and intent.** Ethical hackers work to secure systems, not break them.
