## Why Certifications Demand More Than Study Guides
Earning a penetration-testing certification isn’t about remembering commands — it’s about **thinking and adapting like an attacker**. Exams such as **TryHackMe PT1**, **OSCP**, **eJPT**, and **CompTIA PenTest+** assess your ability to compromise real systems, pivot through networks, and document every step clearly.That’s why traditional study methods — watching tutorials, memorising payloads, reading write-ups — rarely produce strong results. What matters is experience: the ability to recognise familiar patterns, troubleshoot unexpected behaviour, and apply methodology under pressure.Hands-on labs recreate those conditions safely, letting you fail, learn, and try again — until the process becomes second nature.

When you’ve already exploited dozens of vulnerable systems, spotted misconfigurations, and escalated privileges multiple ways, exam day feels familiar — not intimidating.That’s exactly how TryHackMe’s labs are built: interactive, guided, and accessible to anyone with a browser.
