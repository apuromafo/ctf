## Why this matters
Thousands of aspiring cyber learners in Africa  especially women, students or those in disadvantaged communities have passion and potential. What they often lack is access: to devices, to training, to mentorship. CyberSafe works to remove these barriers through initiatives like:
* **CyberGirls Fellowship: **Empowering young women to build cyber careers
* **DigiGirls: **Delivering foundational digital and security skills to girls who otherwise might never get exposure
* **Device Donation Program: **Providing hardware and internet-ready devices for learners who need themThis year, Advent of Cyber doesn’t just build cyber security skills.It helps build **cyber security careers** for those who need the chance most.       Learn more about CyberSafe
