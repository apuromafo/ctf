## Solucionario de Tareas / Task Solutions

> **ES:** El salto de L1 a L2 no es solo técnico: requiere **both** soft y tech skills. Áreas nuevas de L2: threat hunting, escalation, reportes a stakeholderes, working with DFIR. Explorar nuevas áreas de seguridad te hace crecer (**Yea**). La mentalidad clave es la del **attacker mindset**: pensar cómo escalaría y lateralizara un atacante real para ver patrones que el L1 no detecta. El static-site te sitúa en un día real de un L2 con tasks que cambian entre triage, reportes y hunting.
> **EN:** Jumping from L1 to L2 isn't just technical: it requires **both** soft and tech skills. New L2 areas: threat hunting, escalation, stakeholder reporting, DFIR collaboration. Exploring new security areas helps you grow (**Yea**). The critical mindset is the **attacker mindset**: think how a real attacker would escalate and move laterally to spot patterns L1 misses. The static-site puts you in a real L2 day mixing triage, reports and hunting.
