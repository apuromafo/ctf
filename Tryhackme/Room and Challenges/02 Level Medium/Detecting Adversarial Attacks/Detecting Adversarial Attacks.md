## Solucionario de Tareas / Task Solutions

### Task 1 — Introduction to Adversarial Attacks

1.1 What adversarial attack is widely used for testing the robustness of ML models?
> **PGD**

1.2 What adversarial attack spreads perturbations over multiple iterations?
> **BIM**

1.3 What does FGSM stand for?
> **Fast Gradient Sign Method**

### Task 2 — Detecting Adversarial Attacks

2.1 Which attack is usually the hardest to detect by an AI model?
> **PGD**

2.2 Which attack has minimal pixel-level changes?
> **FGSM**
